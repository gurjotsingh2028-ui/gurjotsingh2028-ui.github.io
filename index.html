The paper khata is how India’s neighbourhood shops still run. We are building a way to keep it by voice.

How it works

Three steps. Nothing saved unchecked.

1

Speak or type

Say “रमेश को 500 उधार” in Hindi, or type it. Voice input depends on your phone and browser.

2

Check it

The app shows you the entry first. You confirm or change it before anything is saved.

3

Your khata

Entries land in a ledger with running balances per person. Export it as a CSV file any time.

Being built, not live

  • Sign-up open to every shop owner

Vyapar Voice is an early prototype. We are still working out what shop owners need.

Where the idea came from

It started at a shop counter.

One day I visited a local shop and watched the owner write every transaction in a notebook. I asked why. The answer: it is simply easier, and the software they had seen was expensive and hard to use, even if someone offered it free.

Then I noticed something else. They were sending WhatsApp voice notes all day, including one to a vendor about restocking, because there was no time to type. Their phone already worked by voice. Their accounts did not.

That is where Vyapar Voice began: a khata that fits how shop owners already work.

This is the observation that started the project. It is not a customer study or an endorsement.
